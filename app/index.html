Roadmap to the Platinum

Seven phases, built from the official trophy descriptions and ordered the way the game wants to be played: story first (with the missable warnings up front), then people and activities, then the map, then the grind, then 100%. Roughly: the story is the spine, everything else hangs off it.

  1. Make a manual save before the final mission and keep it. It is cheap insurance for every one-chance moment.
  2. Choose actively. Whenever the game offers a decision, make one; do not let timers expire (see Actions Speak Louder).
  3. Sample everything once early. One try at each activity type banks Master of None and shows you what you enjoy grinding.
  4. Spend and register as you go. Shop spending, vehicle discovery and Scooter Bros registrations all climb for free while you play.

Missable trophies

Most of this list can be earned in any order, at any time, in free roam. The trophies below are the exceptions we flag, conservatively, because their official descriptions imply a one-chance moment or a story choice you cannot revisit. Each card explains exactly why it is flagged.

  1. Keep two save slots: your main save, plus a manual save from before the final story stretch.
  2. Never reload away a failure until Bad Luck, I Guess has popped; a retry you succeed on may be the trophy.
  3. Decide, don't drift: when a choice prompt appears, answer it. Expired choices may not come back.
